The Solution

RecoGST does the matching for you

The hours your team spends cross-checking invoices against GSTR-2B collapse into seconds — and RecoGST doesn't stop at finding problems. It fixes them.

Every Tally entry, imported Unique

It doesn't matter how an entry was made in Tally — purchase voucher, journal voucher, or debit note. RecoGST imports every single one of them, and pulls your GSTR-2B directly from the GST portal or Computax. No copy-paste, no manual entry, no missed invoices.

Exact matches, matched in seconds

Run the reconciliation and watch hundreds of invoices pair up in the blink of an eye — 100% accurate, no formulas, no VLOOKUPs. Duplicates are intercepted before matching even begins, so they can never distort your result.

AI-based suggestions for probable matches

RecoGST tests multiple combinations of invoice data — number, date, GSTIN, and values — to identify invoices that are probably the same pair, even with a typo or a one-day date gap. Approve a suggestion with one click, and your books correct themselves automatically.

Eligible ITC, calculated two ways Unique

Before you file GSTR-3B, RecoGST works out your Eligible ITC twice — once from your books, once from 2B — and lays the two figures side by side. Choose the one that fits your position, accurate or conservative. No other tool offers this.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does RecoGST work with Tally?

Yes — Purchase invoices and Debit/Credit Notes can be imported directly from Tally with one click.

What GST data formats are supported?

GSTR-2B files downloaded from the government portal, and Computax 2B/2A exports. Manual copy-paste is also supported as a fallback.

How accurate is the matching?

Exact invoice matches are 100% automatic. Near-matches (small discrepancies like typos) are surfaced as suggestions for you to review and approve — nothing is changed without your confirmation.

How long does a reconciliation take?

Seconds. Even the complete cycle — importing your data, reconciling every invoice, and calculating Eligible ITC for GSTR-3B — typically finishes in under a minute, even with thousands of invoices.

Can RecoGST calculate Eligible ITC two ways?

Yes — RecoGST calculates Eligible ITC based on your Books data and separately based on your 2B data, and lets you choose which to use for filing.

Do I need to install anything?

RecoGST runs inside Excel — no separate software installation required.
